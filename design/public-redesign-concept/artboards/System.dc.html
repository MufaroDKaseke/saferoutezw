Step 2 · Visual direction

SafeRouteZW — Know the Road Ahead

A premium journey planner with the calm of a public information service. Deep forest green and warm paper replace the generic grey-white. A schematic route line is the one recurring graphic. Photography is reserved for the hero slot and must be locally sourced and licensed; none is used here.

Journey firstThe planner is the front door. Everything else supports a trip.
Words before colourSeverity is always icon + word + colour, and differs in lightness.
Low-data by defaultList first, map on demand, one inline SVG hero, no video, one font family.
Local, not borrowedA3, A4, A5, Harare, Mutare, +263. No foreign highways, no stock incidents.
